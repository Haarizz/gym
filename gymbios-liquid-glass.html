Implementation notes for React Native

  • Use expo-blur's <BlurView intensity={40} tint="light"> for every glass panel — CSS backdrop-filter has no RN equivalent.
  • Glass only looks right with colour behind it: keep the gold/green radial blobs (or a subtle background image) behind scroll content, positioned absolutely under the ScrollView.
  • Panel style: ~40–60% white fill, 1px semi‑transparent white border, soft shadow with the brand colour tinted in at low opacity rather than plain black.
  • Keep solid colour (not glass) only on the two hero cards — active plan and check‑in — so the palette still reads clearly against the frosted neutrals.
  • Respect Reduce Transparency / low-end Android: fall back to solid rgba(255,255,255,0.9) cards when blur isn't available.
